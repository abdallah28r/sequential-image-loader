# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

Sequential Image Loader does **not** collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
- **No network requests.** It never contacts any server. It only changes the order in which the page you are viewing loads its own images.
- **Local settings only.** The list of sites you enabled and your concurrency choice are saved in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`. They never leave your device and are deleted when you remove the extension.
- **Page content.** The extension reads image elements on pages where you turned it on, only to queue them. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
